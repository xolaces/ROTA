Raids drop the parts. The forge makes the piece.

Every set piece now falls from a quest at a grind rate, every raid pays scrap and tack on the damage ladder, and one Reforge recipe per piece turns the two into the better version of what you already wear. This ledger is generated from the same files the server loads.

Before → after

What changed today

Four server fixes and one content pass. Everything below is live on play.riseoftheancients.com.

Quests

  • 2.5–3% early, 0.1–1.4% deep, mounts rarer than the rest→one rate per pool, every piece equal: 1/50 per click in ch.1–2 down to 1/100 in ch.7
  • Marchwatch Tally and Marchwatch Boots dropped nowhere→in the chapter-2 pools with the rest of the set
  • Difficulty still scales ×1.15 / ×1.3 / ×1.5; the boss node still doubles. Deep relics (0.02–0.1%) untouched.

Raids

  • four mounts, one guaranteed per raid, and nothing else→Scrap and Tack for the chapter's sets on every rung; the guaranteed piece stays on the last rung (a mount on most raids; Relay Treads on the chapter-4–5 ones)
  • guild raids dropped nothing→Warlord, Leviathan and Kronarch have ladders
  • every participant banked every rung off one hit→damage-keyed rungs pay on damage (the service only honoured them on World raids)

Forge & profile

  • 96 reforged twins, ×1.5 bonuses, same slot and art; 96 Reforge recipes in their own tab
  • unequip → reforge → re-equip→reforged in place: the slot keeps the better piece
  • profile chips read 0 ATK / 0 DEF→real numbers (Stats was never loaded with the profile)
