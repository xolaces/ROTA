# ROTA — Quest Arc: The Three Fronts

## Overview
This arc binds the major political and military pressure points together into a single chain of escalating events. It provides a way to move the player from local danger into larger world crisis without breaking the continuity of the setting.

The three fronts are:
1. The Ironspine Front
2. The Drowned Coast
3. The Ash March and survivor frontier

Each front carries a different form of danger, but they are all the same problem in different guises: the world is breaking its old boundaries and the factions are struggling to control the fracture.

---

## I. Arc Structure

### Chapter One — The Line Fails
The player begins on the frontier, helping secure a road or keep that looks ordinary but is actually part of a much older defensive network. The first clear sign that something is wrong is a repeating bell signal from the old frontier line. The player learns that a route once considered stable is suddenly active again.

**Quest path:**
- Ashes at the Gate
- Bell of the Last Keep
- Iron in the Bone
- The March That Did Not Return

**Purpose:** reveal the frontier as a wound, not a place.

### Chapter Two — The Drowned Remember
The situation escalates when the player is drawn into the costal ruins and hears reports that the sea is now responding to rituals and old commands. A drowned route or harbor begins to physically resonate with the old war.

**Quest path:**
- Threading the Drowned Road
- The Quiet Beneath the Sea
- The Debt of a Forgotten Crown
- The Lesson of the Black Tide

**Purpose:** reveal the world is not just haunted; it is remembering in active ways.

### Chapter Three — The War Changes Shape
The player discovers that the fighting on the frontier is not merely local. The factions are all trying to exploit the same rupture in different ways. A route, a relic line, or a ruined command site becomes the decisive ground.

**Quest path:**
- The Last Shield of Cengor
- The Veil in the Ironspine
- The Price of a Witness
- The Eye That Holds the Rift

**Purpose:** turn the player from a survivor into a variable in the larger world crisis.

---

## II. Thematic Logic

This arc works because each chapter broadens the same idea:

The world is not suffering random events. The frontier is failing in multiple coordinated places. The old war is not dead. It is reactivating in smaller and more dangerous ways.

---

## III. Why This Arc Matters
The Three Fronts arc gives ROTA a narrative spine. It gives every faction a reason to matter, every region a reason to be meaningful, and every boss or raid a reason to feel tied to a larger crisis instead of existing in isolation.

It also gives the player a progression pattern that matches the game's logic:
- survive the local breach
- encounter the wider wound
- choose what sort of truth to carry forward

---

## IV. Major Cross-Points

### The Iron Weir's decision
Do they hold the frontier at any cost, even if the world becomes brittle under the strain?

### The Dawnward Choir's decision
Do they answer what stirs below the world, even if the answer is not safe?

### The Dusk Ledger's decision
Do they keep profiting from the old world's death, or do they risk losing control by trying to preserve it?

### Malachar's decision
Do they continue the strategy of forced extraction, or does the cost become unacceptable even to them?

These choices do not need to be solved in one arc. They need to be made present.

---

## V. End-state Hook
By the end of the arc, the player has not simply cleared a few areas. They have become evidence that the world is waking up.

The final consequence is not a single boss kill. It is a realization:

The frontier does not need to be won. It needs to be understood before the pressure beneath it becomes impossible to contain.
