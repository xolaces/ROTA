# ROTA — Raid Bank: The Ironspine Front

## Overview
The Ironspine Front is the frontline of the old war. In the mountains, the ancient battlefield does not feel dead. It feels buried beneath a lid.

These raids are built around unstable old-guard structures, old war work, and the sense that the world is not only haunted by the past but actively being rewritten by it. Every boss in this bank is tied to a physical site where the Sundering's shockwave still resonates.

---

## Raid 1: The Last Kiln of Othra
**Theme:** furnace ruin / old-guard military workshop

The old forge still glows with hidden heat beneath a layer of soot and ash. It is rumored that the constructors of the Iron Colossus used this site to test binding alloys and war-engine materials before the Keep fell.

**Encounter tone:** industrial ruin, red heat, collapsing platforms, fire-slag manifestations.

**Loot identity:** old metal relics, ignition sigils, iron-weave armor fragments.

---

## Raid 2: Ironwake at Dorsh
**Theme:** siege line / broken fortification / battlefield rupture

Dorsh was once a chain of fortifications that held the northern front. Now the line is broken and the site keeps drawing combatants back to it like a magnet. The defenders were overrun, but something beneath the rubble still holds the pattern of the attack.

**Encounter tone:** breach and defense, collapsing wall sections, wave combat, war-construct emergence.

**Loot identity:** shield fragments, iron-tower badges, bracers, warline relics.

---

## Raid 3: Cinder-Locked Keep
**Theme:** sealed fortress / old-guard security structure

A fortress built to keep a piece of ruin from escaping. The gates still exist, but the old command logic is breaking down. The defenders are no longer living soldiers; they are the residue of old commands and failed authority.

**Encounter tone:** ceremonial order turned hostile, trap corridors, binding locks, oppressive old-sigil mechanics.

**Loot identity:** seal fragments, null-sigil shards, old warding charms.

---

## Raid 4: The Vein of the Covenant
**Theme:** subterranean old-guard reservoir / binding engine chamber

Beneath the mountain, old channel systems still conduct resonance between ruin sites. This raid is a deep-chamber fight through cut stone, collapsed channels, and a live resonance core that keeps reacting to the player's presence.

**Encounter tone:** claustrophobic, rhythmic, echo-heavy, pressure-driven.

**Loot identity:** binding plates, veined armor, inhibitor relics, resonance amulets.

---

## Raid 5: The Pale Warden
**Theme:** gate guardian / spectral survivor of the Long Watch

The Warden was once a champion of the old frontier, tasked with holding a path no army could hold. It remains at that job even after death, still enforcing the old rule that no one enters without the correct response.

**Encounter tone:** ritual duel, spectral phasing, weapon-skill patterning, old-sigil authority.

**Loot identity:** warding swords, funerary cloaks, rimed sigils, relic keys.

---

## Raid 6: Bellwether of the Red Pass
**Theme:** mountain pass siege / failing border threshold

The Red Pass still bears the marks of a battle that happened long before the current age. The land remains scarred, and the pass has become a place where old commands keep manifesting. It is not simply a ruined route; it is a battlefield still making itself active.

**Encounter tone:** wide terrain, layered fights, ambush pressure, rising battlefield hazard effects.

**Loot identity:** pass-seal armor, broken banners, command medallions, front-line relics.

---

## Raid 7: The Gloam Span
**Theme:** broken bridge / crossing point / old front line

A ruined bridge over a ravine still vibrates with the old war's shock. Crossing it feels like stepping into a place where the world has determined to keep killing the same battle again.

**Encounter tone:** narrow lanes, falling debris, mobility pressure, heavy defensive rhythm.

**Loot identity:** chainmail, bridge-tether charms, horizon sigils, broken standards.

---

## Raid 8: The Seventh Ledger
**Theme:** archive under siege / relic debt and false memory

An old-guard vault built to record the losses of the frontier. The records remain, but some entries are not names. They are warnings. The chamber has become a trap for anyone who believes it is only a storage site.

**Encounter tone:** archive horror, looping corridors, incomplete records, memory-fracture threats.

**Loot identity:** recorded seals, relic ledgers, debt-currency charms, memory-worn armor.

---

## Boss Family Pattern
The Ironspine Front is intentionally built around a pattern:
- gatekeepers who were never meant to stop living enemies
- war-constructs still enforcing old commands
- relic-bearers who carry the logic of the old frontier into the present
- mountain spirits or fading manifestations whose purpose is tied to the line itself

This makes the zone feel like a real old battlefield, not just a random dungeon collection.

---

## Why This Bank Works
This raid bank gives ROTA a clear mountain-front identity. It is broad enough to support progression, but coherent enough to support a long arc in which the frontier starts to fail and the old war beneath the world begins to leak upward.

It also sharpens the difference between ROTA and DOTD by emphasizing procedural old-war ruin, defensive identity, and a long-simmering frontier pressure rather than only a single high-contrast boss fantasy.
