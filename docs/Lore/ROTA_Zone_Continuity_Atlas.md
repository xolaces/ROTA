# ROTA — Zone Continuity Atlas

## Purpose
This document preserves the relationship between quest zones, their story source, and the raids that emerge from those places. The goal is to keep the world legible: players should feel that a raid came from a place, and that place has a story that began in a quest.

The world setting is not a random list of boss names. Each major zone should feel like a layered memory: the quest line reveals the place, the raid expands it, and the boss embodies the haunting force that made it dangerous.

---

## I. Ironspine Frontline

### Zone: The Last Keep Line
- Quest arc origin: "Bell of the Last Keep", "Broken Oath, Iron Gate", "The Last Shield of Cengor"
- Narrative source: the old frontier barrier between the Sundermarch and the Ironspine ruins
- Raid extension: "Cinder-Locked Keep", "Bellwether of the Red Pass", "The Pale Warden"
- Boss logic: old-guard gatekeepers and war-signatures still enforcing failed commands

### Zone: The Steel Ruin March
- Quest arc origin: "Iron in the Bone", "Ashes at the Gate", "The March That Did Not Return"
- Narrative source: the broken supply and assault roads of the old war
- Raid extension: "Ironwake at Dorsh", "The Gloam Span", "The Grief Engine"
- Boss logic: battlefield remnants, failed routs, and abandoned command lines

### Zone: The Hollow Forge Wells
- Quest arc origin: "The Veil in the Ironspine", "The Unlit Pyre", "A Relic for the Quiet"
- Narrative source: old-guard industrial and signal-harvest sites beneath the mountains
- Raid extension: "The Last Kiln of Othra", "The Vein of the Covenant", "The Seventh Ledger"
- Boss logic: old war-engine chambers and archive-furnaces carrying impossible memory

## III. Frontier Rule: Place-first Worldbuilding

When building or revising lore, follow this rule:

- Quest lines establish the place, the wound, and the emotional logic.
- Raids expand the place into active danger.
- Bosses personify the force that made the place dangerous.
- Loot and relic lines are the consequence of what that place has held or buried.

This keeps the world from becoming a disconnected collection of dungeon names.

---

## IV. A Good Example of Alignment

### Example: The Bell of the Last Keep
- Quest hook: a frontier town hears a bell under the mountain that should not be ringing.
- Place identity: an old defensive line that is failing because it was built to ward against a threat no the living world remembers.
- Raid extension: "Cinder-Locked Keep" and "Bellwether of the Red Pass"
- Boss logic: older command systems that still enforce a forgotten war directive.

This works because the quest introduces the place, the raid reveals the place under active pressure, and the boss is the literal embodiment of the place's unresolved command.

---

## V. Naming Convention Guidance

A good ROTA zone should use naming that reflects its origin, even when the raid title changes.

Prefer:
- same region terminology across quest and raid titles
- shared terrain cues: keep, pass, harbor, forge, coast, line, vault, bell, tether, stair, gate
- consistent emotional vocabulary: iron, salt, ash, glass, drowned, broken, quiet, sealed, black, cinder, hollow

Avoid:
- purely random mythology names with no place anchor
- a raid that sounds like it belongs to a different region entirely
- a boss whose identity has nothing to do with the scenic logic of the zone

---

## VI. Continuity Pattern by Content Type

### Quests reveal the wound
"Ashes at the Gate" reveals a place where a line failed.

### Raids reveal the consequence
"Cinder-Locked Keep" shows what happened after the line failed.

### Bosses embody the force
The Pale Warden is not a random boss; it is what the gate became after the line kept fighting beyond human death.

### Loot reveals the residue
The relics, seals, wards, and salts are the physical afterimage of the event.

---

## VII. Expansion Principles for Future Content

When creating new regions, use this template:

1. Zone name and threat origin
2. quest arc that explains why the place matters
3. raid family that turns the place into active danger
4. boss family that personifies the place's historical wound
5. relic line that carries the memory or consequence of the event

This keeps every new zone coherent and immediately usable for gameplay.
