# ROTA — Quest-to-Raid Continuity Pairs

## Purpose
This is the practical mapping layer for the world. Each entry links a quest origin, a zone identity, a raid expansion, a boss, and the relic residue left behind.

---

## A. The Ironspine Frontier

### Pair 1: Bell of the Last Keep
- Quest hook: "Bell of the Last Keep"
- Place: the abandoned outer line of the Ironspine frontier
- Raid expansion: "Cinder-Locked Keep"
- Boss: The Pale Warden
- Why it fits: a fortified line that still obeys an old command no one alive remembers issuing
- Relic residue: seal fragments, old warding plates, ghosted command staves

### Pair 2: Ashes at the Gate
- Quest hook: "Ashes at the Gate"
- Place: the broken siege road and ruined gatehouse
- Raid expansion: "Ironwake at Dorsh"
- Boss: Vhar the Gatebreaker
- Why it fits: the battle line was broken in a single terrible rush and never properly buried
- Relic residue: banner iron, cracked shield bands, ash-etched troves

### Pair 3: The Veil in the Ironspine
- Quest hook: "The Veil in the Ironspine"
- Place: mountain-side signal chambers and hidden forge routes
- Raid expansion: "The Vein of the Covenant"
- Boss: The Mantle of Othra
- Why it fits: a deep mechanical chamber built to carry resonance beneath the mountain
- Relic residue: null-sigil threads, iron-chord bindings, resonance carvings

### Pair 4: The Last Shield of Cengor
- Quest hook: "The Last Shield of Cengor"
- Place: a final line of retreat built in the pass itself
- Raid expansion: "Bellwether of the Red Pass"
- Boss: The Red Warden
- Why it fits: the pass still functions as a battlefield even after the defenders are gone
- Relic residue: red-ash standard poles, war-sign shields, pass-seal buckles

## D. Rules for New Content Generation

When creating new quest or raid content, always answer these questions:

1. What place is this about?
2. Why did the place become dangerous?
3. What kind of old event happened there?
4. What still remains after the event?
5. What boss would embody the event?
6. What relic line would the place leave behind?

If the answers are clear, the content feels connected. If not, it becomes random.

## E. Recommended Continuity Pattern for the Next Pass
Focus on three new zone groups:
- a ruined mountain pilgrimage line
- a flood-torn merchant coast
- a spiritual ruin where the spoken name of a place has become a weapon

For each zone, produce:
- 2 quest hooks
- 1 raid family
- 1 boss role
- 1 relic line
- 1 faction tension node

That gives ROTA a strong world engine while maintaining the map logic.
