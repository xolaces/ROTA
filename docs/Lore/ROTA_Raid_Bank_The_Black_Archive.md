# ROTA — Raid Bank: The Black Archive

## Overview
The Black Archive is not a single site. It is a network of sealed command sanctums, hidden relay vaults, and fractured record halls spread beneath the old frontier line. These places are not mysterious because they are unreachable. They are mysterious because they were meant to remain untouched and still carry the logic of their original purpose.

The Black Archive raids are built around old-guard continuity systems: records, seals, relay logic, and command-systems that refused to die even after the war did.

---

## Raid 1: The Relay of Sable Glass
**Theme:** signal tower / memory relay / dead communication network

This site once relayed warnings across the frontier. It still receives something from the dark in intervals, not as noise, but as a pattern. The tower is not empty; it is operating by a logic no living operator understands.

**Encounter tone:** echo-chamber signals, repeating warnings, phased defenders, old alarm mechanics.

**Loot identity:** relay cores, line-ward sigils, warning bands, beacon glass shards.

---

## Raid 2: The Quiet Wind Vault
**Theme:** sealed archive / command record hall

Built beneath a now-collapsed frontier outpost, this vault stores route maps, line failures, and casualty records from the final collapse of the frontier defense system. It is less haunted than it is full of unfinished decisions.

**Encounter tone:** stacked archive rooms, dead light, pressure from memory-scarred enemies, false route paths.

**Loot identity:** archive seals, witness-ink charms, line-keeper plates, dead route maps.

---

## Raid 3: The Dead Lantern Chain
**Theme:** beacon corridor / watchline progression

This chain of abandoned lantern towers still emits a signal in the dark. It is worked by a rotating system of dead watchers who continue to perform their duties even after the war ended. The players who enter it are not just fighting enemies; they are entering a system that never stopped functioning.

**Encounter tone:** multi-room progression, signal hazards, long-range pressure, corridor traps.

**Loot identity:** lantern-metal armor, relay chains, signal discs, warding rinses.

---

## Raid 4: The Threnody Vault of Hallowglass
**Theme:** memory vault / sealed archive / witness chamber

This location is not purely military. It is a threshold chamber where old-guard records are stored under ritual protection. It is dangerous because the records still react to humans. Some of them are not dead data. They are still listening.

**Encounter tone:** ritualized archive pressure, mirrored surfaces, dissonant voice patterns, false door logic.

**Loot identity:** glass witness relics, archive keys, line-keeper medallions, old access tokens.

---

## Raid 5: The Last Lamp of Arveth
**Theme:** sentinel beacon / final relay site

This tower was the final hold-out signal before the frontier truly collapsed. It still burns with a cold, impossible light. Many who enter it are not killed by combat; they are overwhelmed by the sense that the site is still waiting for someone to confirm the line still exists.

**Encounter tone:** high-pressure defense, ritual flare hazards, layered watch-pattern attacks.

**Loot identity:** arclight relics, shutter plates, old command rings, signal-gold pins.

---

## Raid 6: The Null Corridor
**Theme:** sealed route / threshold denial / command fracture

This corridor is a dead route that should not be active. It remains active under a false state, as if the line never accepted that it had collapsed. It is one of the most dangerous sites in the Black Archive network because the people who survive it are never fully sure whether they passed through a passage or a decision.

**Encounter tone:** repeating geometry, false route choices, hostile threshold logic, pressure from impossible movement.

**Loot identity:** threshold seals, dead-route tokens, broken authority charms, route-keys.

---

## Boss Logic of the Black Archive
The bosses in this raid bank are less monsters than inherited functions:
- relay wardens
- dead lantern commanders
- route executors
- sealed keepers
- line-analysts who still execute the old command set

These are not random boss archetypes; they are structural embodiments of a system that tried to keep the border alive too long.

---

## Why This Bank Matters
The Black Archive fills the gap between the frontier war and the deeper old-world mysteries. It provides content for players who want to push further into the logic of the Old Guard without immediately stepping into a pure mythic or cult arc.

It is the practical, institutional answer to the question: what did the Old Guard leave behind besides ruins and relics?

The answer is continuity infrastructure.
