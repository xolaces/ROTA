# ROTA Content Production Plan

This file defines the autonomous content production loop for the ROTA world-building stream.

## Primary agent
- rota-content-architect

## Mission
Continue expanding ROTA's world content while preserving the canon and creating a strong DotD-like mood with a different identity.

## Core design principle
ROTA should feel like a sibling to DOTD in tone and pressure, but not a copy. The world should retain the same sense of ancient dread, frontiers, ruin, and escalating danger, while differentiating itself through its own factions, rituals, politics, and haunting world logic.

## Monthly content targets
- 2 faction clusters
- 1 major regional arc
- 1 commander roster
- 1 raid bank
- 1 relic and gear expansion pass
- 1 chapter/quest chain
- 1 major lore conflict summary

## Weekly operating loop
1. Read canonical lore files.
2. Identify a gap in the world.
3. Generate a dense content batch.
4. Save output in docs/Lore/.
5. Update the next content queue.
6. Validate internal cohesion against existing canon.

## Content bank categories
- factions and political blocs
- commanders and war leaders
- region and frontier summaries
- raid names and themes
- quests and chapter arcs
- gear families and drop names
- relics and artifacts
- social and spiritual movements
- callouts for world events and escalation hooks

## Best practices
- Build names that feel etched, not copied.
- Prefer world logic over lore dumps.
- Tie every new content batch to the underlying pressure of Awakening Essence.
- Make each faction feel economically and ideologically motivated.
- Give each raid a reason to exist and a cost to the world.
- Let the item and relic lines tell a story instead of acting as isolated tables.

## Content quality checklist
Before closing a content batch, confirm:
- it fits the Aldenmor canon
- it does not feel generic or derivative
- it introduces distinctive identity
- it can support gameplay progression
- it deepens emotional or political tension
- it provides future content hooks

## Next generation queue
Priority order for future work:
1. Frontier kingdoms and regional politics
2. A major Malachar countercampaign
3. Old Guard survivor organizations
4. Ancient-specific cult and awakening rites
5. A full raid and boss family expansion around the Drowned Coast
6. A full set of faction rivalries and war fronts
7. A larger relic hierarchy and secret-order lore layer

## Output conventions
Use filenames like:
- ROTA_Faction_[Name].md
- ROTA_Region_[Name].md
- ROTA_Raid_Bank_[Theme].md
- ROTA_Quest_Arc_[Title].md
- ROTA_Relic_Line_[Name].md

The goal is not to work in a single burst. The goal is to create a persistent world engine that keeps producing useful content without losing coherence.
