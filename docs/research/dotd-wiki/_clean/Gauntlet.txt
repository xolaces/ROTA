mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"> Gauntlet is a limited-time event in which players use Gauntlet Strikes to attack Gauntlet Raids, receiving 10 Strikes and 1 Gauntlet Token per raid defeated. Tokens can be spent in the Gauntlet Shop .

There are 3 leagues, separating players by level: Whelpling 
(20-999), Wyrm (1000-2499) and Dragon (2500+). The top 500 players in 
each league win special prizes.
