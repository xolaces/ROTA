Planet Coins are a special currency used to purchase unique/special 
items and services in game, the planet coin cost will be displayed next 
to the item with the symbol. Examples of these items are the limited edition bazaar equipment/consumables, stamina refills, class/name changes.

Your current available number of Planet Coins is displayed in the top right section of the menu bar.

pictures will be added at a later time. (3 variants) - Lorak990 17:25, December 14, 2010 (UTC)

 Completing surveys
