Expedition Rarity [ ]

Each tier in the expeditions represents the chance of gaining 
something from that tier. There is a 10% chance to gain from tier 1, 20%
 from tier 2, 30% from tier 3 and 40% from tier 4. The exact chances of 
the individual items in each tier vary slightly, with the items to the 
left being rarer than the ones to the right.
