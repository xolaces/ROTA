# ROTA — Privacy Policy (Beta)

**Version 1 — placeholder. Replace with reviewed legal text before public beta.**

1. **What we collect.** Email address, username, password (stored only as a salted BCrypt hash),
   gameplay state, chat messages, and IP addresses in security audit logs.
2. **Why.** To operate the game (authentication, progress, social features), prevent abuse (rate
   limiting, audit logging, moderation), and to send account emails (e.g. password reset codes).
3. **Email.** Your address is used for login identity and transactional mail only — no marketing,
   never shared or sold.
4. **Retention.** Audit logs are append-only and retained for the life of the service. Account
   deletion soft-deletes your profile; ledger rows required for integrity are retained.
5. **Third parties.** Email delivery is performed via the operator's SMTP provider; data is hosted
   with the operator's infrastructure provider. No analytics or advertising trackers.
6. **Contact.** Questions or deletion requests: operator@example.com.
